## The concept whole

A novel is not a manuscript. It is a **system** — a simulation run in the mind through
what A.G. calls *novelistic phenomenology*. The design concept for a novel can be produced
in a microsecond; the writing is the slow execution of the system. The design concept is
necessarily **plural**: it can take a multitude of forms, and no single form exhausts it.
Dozens of novels designed over the years, most of them unfinished — not from lack of
vision but from a fatal flaw in the system itself (see Bottleneck note).

## Systems and simulations

- **Novelistic phenomenology**: the novel as a simulation one runs in one's mind; the
  concept is grasped whole before a word is written.
- **Databases-of-images** (since 1998): novels/novellas treated as image-banks rather
  than linear narratives — an early form of the system.
- **Characterology**: a complete characterology is prerequisite; each character arrives
  with its own aesthetic/design problem, which must be worked out in the head to be
  understood. "We ARE naked problems, white signs on a blackboard" (Salmon on Picasso's
  *Demoiselles*).
- **The (Cultural) Software Library**: the unit of production is not the novel but the
  **facet** — one module per day, committed to the library, across many novels at once.

## Philosophical machinery

- **Design science**: design principles learned in Computer-Assisted Sound Design,
  applied identically to writing and visual art — "it's the same thing for any
  discipline, mode of expression, or genre."
- **Livingry vs. weaponry** (Fuller): designers are engineers of *livingry*; livingry
  heals the wound of History — the **historiopathies** (historical pathologies), at the
  microscopic scale of a career full of catastrophic failures. Design was the way out of
  the labyrinth, every time.
- **The First Witness**: "I alone am the First Witness of my life" — testimony is
  limited and imperfect, so it refers back to the Records (the Refcard system), which
  cannot lie; interpretation then becomes a problem of **hermeneutic triangulation**.
- **The Historiotheque as Witness-Box**: the studio conceived as a witness-box and a
  **Temple of Memory** — a design concept for a historical art-house, passed down so
  that the Invisible Forces of History are balanced by a distributed ledger of designs
  across time and space. Cf. Otlet's Mundaneum as a living archive.

## Bottleneck note

The fatal flaw: the novels were designed **far too grand in scale** — each demanding
~1000 pages, hence dozens of unfinished novels in varying states of disrepair, each
abandonment a small death of the artist's soul. The patch is **encapsulation**: shorter
pieces, one facet/module per day per novel, many novels in parallel. "I can't finish a
1000-page novel in one sitting, but I can write (design the concepts of) 1000 novels in
one sitting." The arithmetic of the patch: 10 pages/day = 3,650 pages/year = 36,500 in
ten years — more than enough to finish everything extant. Grand vision ("holy ambition
for the sacred journey," the Cathedral of Dreams) is kept; grand *units of delivery*
are scrapped.

## Open questions

- How do the facets compose back into wholes? What governs the assembly of modules into
  a novel?
- The hermeneutic triangulation problem: if the records cannot lie but the witness can
  misremember, what is the status of the simulation?
- The fear of interruption: history and grief have repeatedly scrapped the work
  mid-course. Can the modular system survive interruption — is a facet-library
  inherently more interruption-proof than a manuscript?
- Textbook Art, the Surrealist Textbook, the Abstract Handbook, the massive Glossary
  of Art History: are these novels, databases, or something else the system hasn't
  named yet?
